## App name (max 30)
Roshan Khata – Udhaar Ledger

## Short description (max 80)
Shop khata, counter sale, stock and bills. Offline, in your language.

## Full description (max 4000)
Roshan Khata is the shop's ledger and counter, on your phone. Customer udhaar, the day's sales, cash, cheques, supplier bills and stock — kept the way they are kept on paper, in the language your shop is run in.

Made for the small shop and the wholesaler: the kiryana store, the medical store, the mobile shop, the hardware shop, the agri dealer and more — 25 shop types. Choose yours once and the app speaks your trade.

KHATA — WHO OWES WHAT
• Every customer's account with a running balance
• "I gave" and "I got" in one tap, with the goods and quantity
• Find anyone by name or number, or just say it — voice entry in Urdu, English, Hindi and more
• A customer statement as PDF, shared on WhatsApp in a second
• Follow-up: who to remind today, with reminders that grow firmer step by step from your own WhatsApp
• Payment Radar: how each customer usually pays, worked out on your phone from your own khata

COUNTER — SELL FAST
• Ring up a sale for a walk-in or a khata customer, with the camera as the barcode scanner
• Cash, bank, cheque or wallet, or part on account
• Returns that put the goods back on the shelf, with a slip for the customer
• Receipts on a Bluetooth printer; the day report and close of day

STOCK, BILLS AND DOCUMENTS
• Products with their prices, barcode and place on the shelf; a price check from the barcode
• Batches and expiry: what expires first is sold first; the stock register
• Supplier bills, purchase orders and what you still owe
• Quotation, sales order, delivery challan and invoice, each with its own number; 10 printed designs with your logo, signature and QR
• Price list pictures, ready to send on WhatsApp

CASHBOOK, CHEQUES AND PLANS
• Cash in and cash out, by category, and what should be in the drawer
• Post-dated cheques with due-date reminders
• Instalment plans, with the next due date in view

STAFF
• Owner, manager and cashier, each with their own PIN
• A record of who did what; what a cashier may not do alone waits for a manager's PIN

YOUR BOOKS, YOUR PHONE
• No account, no sign-up, no ads — the app works fully offline
• Your ledger stays on your phone and we never collect it. It leaves only when you choose: a backup to your own Google Drive, or a copy sent to your own device
• App lock with your fingerprint or PIN; hide the balance when someone is looking
• Several shops? Each business keeps its own separate book

NINE LANGUAGES
English, Urdu, Roman Urdu, Sindhi, Arabic, Persian, Hindi, Bengali and Indonesian — every screen, every receipt. Your shop's own currency, calendar and WhatsApp code.
