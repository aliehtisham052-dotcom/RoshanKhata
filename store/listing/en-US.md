## Full description (max 4000)
Roshan Khata is the shop counter's ledger, on your phone. Customer udhaar, daily cash, cheques, supplier bills and instalments — kept the way they are kept on paper, in the language your shop is run in.

Built for the small shopkeeper in Pakistan: the agri-chemical dealer, the kiryana store, the medical store, the mobile shop, the hardware shop and more. Choose your shop type once and the app speaks your trade.

KHATA — WHO OWES WHAT
• Every customer's account with a running balance
• "I gave" and "I got" in one tap, with the goods and quantity
• Find anyone by name or number, or just say it — voice entry in Urdu, English, Hindi and more
• A customer statement as PDF, shared on WhatsApp in a second
• Follow-up: see who is paying later than usual

CASHBOOK — TODAY'S POSITION
• Cash in and cash out, by category
• Daily, weekly and monthly totals without a calculator
• A cash-flow outlook from your plans, cheques and bills

CHEQUES, BILLS AND PLANS
• Post-dated cheques with due-date reminders
• Supplier bills and what you still owe
• Instalment plans, with the next due date in view

INVOICES AND PRODUCTS
• Printed-quality invoices in 10 designs, with your logo, signature and QR
• Products and stock, with expiry alerts for dated goods
• Price list and purchase rates, ready to share

YOUR BOOKS, YOUR PHONE
• No account, no sign-up, no ads — the app works fully offline
• Nothing is collected or sent anywhere; your data stays on your phone
• Backup to your own Google Drive, automatic if you want it
• App lock with your fingerprint or PIN; hide the balance when someone is looking
• Several shops? Each business keeps its own separate book

NINE LANGUAGES
English, Urdu, Roman Urdu, Sindhi, Arabic, Persian, Hindi, Bengali and Indonesian — every screen, every receipt.
