line: One day Sulaiman looked over the birds and did not see me. He said: “Why do I not see the hoopoe? Is he among the absent? I will punish him severely, or slaughter him, unless he brings me a clear reason” (27:20–21).

line: “I found a woman ruling over them; she has been given of everything, and she has a great throne” (27:23).
line: “I found her and her people bowing to the sun instead of Allah. Allah — there is no god but Him, the Lord of the Great Throne” (27:24–26).
line: Sulaiman answered: “We shall see whether you have spoken the truth or are of the liars. Take this letter of mine, deliver it to them, then draw back from them and see what they send back” (27:27–28).

line: She asked her chiefs for counsel. They answered that they were strong and mighty in war, and the command was hers. She said that kings who enter a town ruin it and humble its honoured people, and that she would send a gift and see what the envoys bring back (27:32–35).
line: When her envoy came to Sulaiman, he said: “Do you help me with wealth? What Allah has given me is better than what He has given you. It is you who rejoice in your gift. Go back to them” (27:36–37). Then one who had knowledge of the Book said he would bring her throne before the blink of an eye — and when Sulaiman saw it standing before him, he said: “This is by the favour of my Lord, to test me whether I am grateful or ungrateful” (27:38–40).

And the Quran closes the account with her own words: “My Lord, I have wronged myself, and I submit with Sulaiman to Allah, the Lord of the worlds”. That is the outcome.
