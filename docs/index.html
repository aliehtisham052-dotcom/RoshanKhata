What the app does

Khata — who owes what

Every customer's account with a running balance. "I gave" and "I got" in one tap, with goods and quantity. Voice entry, and a PDF statement for WhatsApp.

Cashbook

Cash in and cash out by category, with daily, weekly and monthly totals.

Cheques, bills, plans

Post-dated cheques with reminders, supplier bills and what you still owe, and instalment plans.

Invoices and stock

Invoices in 10 designs with your logo and signature, products and stock with expiry alerts, and a price list to share.

Your books stay on your phone

  • No account, no sign-up and no ads. The app works fully offline.
  • Your records are stored on your own device. We run no server and collect nothing.
  • Backup is optional and goes to your own Google Drive, into the app's private folder only. The app cannot see your other Drive files.
  • App lock with fingerprint or PIN.

Read exactly what the app does with your information in the Privacy Policy.

Nine languages

English, Urdu, Roman Urdu, Sindhi, Arabic, Persian, Hindi, Bengali and Indonesian — every screen and every receipt.
