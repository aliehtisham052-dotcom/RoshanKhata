Roshan Khata — Privacy Policy

Har Hisaab Roshan · By Innovation-313

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · اردو میں پڑھیں

The short version: Roshan Khata keeps your business records on your own phone. We do not run a server, we do not collect your ledger, and we never sell or share your data. Cloud backup, if you turn it on, goes to your own Google Drive — not to us.

1. Who we are

Roshan Khata ("the app") is a digital ledger (bahi-khata) for small businesses, built by Innovation-313. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Contact: innovation313.support@gmail.com

2. What data the app stores

The app stores the business records you enter — customer and supplier names, phone numbers, transactions, balances, cheques, payment plans, invoices, supplier bills (with batch numbers and expiry dates), products and stock, crop seasons, company schemes, and, if you use it, your staff's names, attendance and pay. It also keeps your shop's own profile: its name, address, phone, email, tax number, logo, stamp, signature and payment QR. All of this is kept locally on your device. It is not sent to us, and we have no access to it.

3. More than one business

You can keep several businesses in the app — a second shop, or a separate book for a separate trade. Each one is stored in a completely separate file on your phone. One business cannot see another's customers, entries, photos or invoices, and each has its own backup. Nothing is shared between them except the app's own settings, such as the language and the app lock.

4. Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app can back up your records so you can recover them if you change or lose your phone. Please note:

  • The backup goes to your own Google Drive, using your Google account. It does not come to us.
  • The backup is stored in the app's own private folder on your Drive (appDataFolder). The app cannot see or touch any of your other Drive files, documents, or photos.
  • The app requests only one Drive permission — access to its own app-data folder — and nothing more.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive at any time from within the app.
  • We do not read, collect, store, or share the contents of your backup. It stays in your Drive, under your control.

5. Google user data, and how it is used

When you connect Google Drive, you sign in with your Google account. From Google, the app receives:

  • Your Google account's email address — used only to show you, on the Backup screen, which account your backup goes to (for example "Connected as name@gmail.com"), so a backup to the wrong account can be noticed. It is remembered only on your phone.
  • Access to the app's own folder in your Drive (drive.appdata) — used only to save, list and restore your own backup files.

This data is used for nothing else. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, not used for advertising, not sold, and not read by any person. You can remove the app's access at any time by disconnecting inside the app, or from your Google Account's security settings.

Roshan Khata's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Helper's read-only copy

A shop owner can let a helper see the books on the helper's own phone. The helper's phone opens a copy — from a backup file the owner sends, or from the owner's own Drive backup. That copy is read-only: the helper's phone cannot change any entry and never uploads anything. The owner decides who gets a copy.

7. Daily copies kept on your phone

Once a day, when you open the ledger, the app quietly copies that business's records into its own private storage on your phone, and keeps the last seven days. This is protection against a damaged file — nothing more. These copies never leave your phone, are not uploaded anywhere, and are not part of any backup you share. You can see them, and go back to any of them, from Backup → Daily copies.

8. File backup

The app can also save a backup file to your phone's Downloads, which you can then share (for example over WhatsApp) or keep yourself. This file stays on your device and wherever you choose to send it. We are not involved.

9. Contacts import (optional)

You can bring customers in from your phone's contact list instead of typing them. The app reads your contacts only when you tap Import, takes just the name and phone number of the people you pick, and saves them as customers on your phone. Your contact list is never uploaded to us or to anyone. You can take the Contacts permission back at any time in your phone's settings — the app keeps working, you would simply add customers by hand.

10. Photos you attach

You can attach a photo to a customer (their face, so you recognise them) and a photo of a bill to an entry. Both are taken with your phone's own camera app, or picked from your gallery, and are stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded to us. A customer's photo is deliberately kept off statements — a statement gets forwarded, and your customer did not agree to their face travelling with it.

11. Voice entry

If you use the microphone button to speak an entry, the listening is done by your phone's own Google speech service, which converts your words to text and hands the text to the app — Google states this on its own dialog. The app keeps no recording of your voice, and nothing is written into your ledger from a spoken sentence without you seeing and confirming it first.

12. Customer QR cards

You can give a customer a QR card that opens their ledger when you scan it. The code on the card carries no name, no phone number and no balance — only a random tag that means something inside the app on your phone and nowhere else. A lost or forwarded card tells its finder nothing. Scanning uses the scanner built into Google Play services on your phone.

13. Receipt printer (Bluetooth, optional)

You can print a receipt on a Bluetooth receipt printer that you have already paired in your phone's Bluetooth settings. The app does not scan for devices and does not use your location. Only the receipt itself is sent, and only to your own printer.

14. Business documents you make and share

Invoices, receipts, statements, business cards, price lists and Status posters (pictures and short videos) are all drawn on your phone, from the details you entered. They leave your phone only when you share, print or save them — for example over WhatsApp — and only to the people you choose. A customer's signature taken on a receipt is stored on your phone with that receipt.

15. Scanning a supplier's bill

If you scan a printed bill, the photo is taken with Google's document scanner and its text is read by Google ML Kit text recognition. Both run on your phone through Google Play services; the bill's image and its text are not sent to us. Like other Google Play services features, ML Kit sends Google basic technical information — such as the device model, Android version, the app's name and version, how long a scan took, and error codes — which Google uses for diagnostics and to improve the service. It does not include your bills, your customers or your ledger. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

16. Roshan Khata for Windows

Roshan Khata also runs on a Windows computer. There, too, the book is kept only on that computer, in your own Windows user folder, with a daily copy beside it and, if you choose, a second copy in a folder you pick (a USB stick, or a Google Drive for Desktop folder). Nothing is sent to us. Once a day the Windows app reads a small version file from the app's own public download page to tell you when a newer version is available; it sends nothing about you or your book to do so. A crash is written to a log on your computer, and leaves it only if you choose Report a problem and send it.

17. Moving the book between your phone and computer

The phone and the computer can send each other a whole copy of the book over your own Wi-Fi. The computer shows a code; the phone scans it; the copy goes directly between the two devices, sealed with a one-time key from that code (AES-GCM), and is handed over once per code. It does not pass through the internet or through us. Android's own phone-to-phone transfer and Android's own cloud backup do not copy the book: it moves only by the app's own backup, which you choose.

Live sync (optional). If you join the phone to the computer for live sync, each change goes between them over your Wi-Fi, sealed with the book's own key (AES-256-GCM). If you also turn on "Also through the internet", changes made while the phone is away from the shop's Wi-Fi pass through Innovation 313's relay in that sealed form (on Cloudflare; when it cannot be reached, on Innovation 313's own web hosting): no relay can read them, and each keeps them for at most 7 days. Several devices of one shop (for example your phone, your staff's phone and the computer) share the same book this way. The book's key stays on your devices, sealed by the phone's own key store and, on the computer, by Windows for your user account; it is never sent to a relay. While the app is closed, the relay may ask Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver a message to your phone: either one word ("rk-wake"), so the phone picks up what is waiting, or a small change itself, still sealed with the book's key, which Google carries but cannot read. For this, Firebase gives the phone a token, and Google receives a Firebase installation ID, the app's version and basic device information. No name, figure or other part of the book is sent to Google in a form it can read. None of this happens until you turn the relay on, and turning it off on the phone deletes the token.

18. Staff sign-in and the activity log

If you let staff use the counter, each person you add signs in with their own PIN. PINs are never stored as typed — only as a salted, one-way hash — and the book keeps a log of who did what (a sale, a void, a return, an edit or delete, an export), so you can answer for it later. The PINs' hashes and the log are part of the book, so they travel with your own backups and stay under your control.

19. Reminders and rating the app

Reminders (a backup being due, a payment you promised to chase) are scheduled on your phone with Android's own job scheduler, and are set again after the phone restarts. Now and then the app may ask, through Google Play's own in-app review window, whether you would like to rate it; anything you write there goes to Google Play, not to us.

20. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or partners.
  • We do not use your ledger for analytics or profiling, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDK of ours.
  • We do not track your location.

21. Permissions the app uses

  • Internet (and checking the network's state) — only to upload/download your backup to your own Google Drive, when you turn cloud backup on.
  • Contacts (read) — only when you tap Import, to bring the customers you pick into the app, as described above. Never uploaded. On newer Android versions the phone's own contact picker is used instead, and no Contacts permission is needed at all.
  • Bluetooth (connect) — only to send a receipt to a printer you have already paired. No scanning and no location.
  • Run at start-up, keep awake briefly, background work (added by Android's own scheduler) — only so that reminders and the automatic backup still happen after the phone restarts.
  • Notifications — only for the app's own reminders on your phone, such as a backup being due.
  • Google Drive (app-data folder only) — to store your backup in your own Drive, as described above.
  • Fingerprint / device lock — only to lock the app on your device, if you enable App Lock. This never leaves your phone.
  • No camera permission, no microphone permission. Photos are taken by your phone's own camera app, speech is heard by your phone's own Google dialog, and QR scanning runs inside Google Play services — so the app itself never holds the right to your camera or microphone.

22. Your rights

  • You can delete any customer, entry, bill or invoice from within the app. Deleted items wait in the Recycle Bin for 30 days, so a mistake can be undone, and are then removed for good.
  • You can erase everything on this phone at once with Backup → Erase all data. Your own Drive backup is left for you to keep or delete.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive at any time.
  • You can delete the backup file from your Drive or your phone yourself.
  • Because everything is stored on your own phone and nothing is held on any server of ours, uninstalling the app removes all of it — including the daily copies. Your Drive backup, if you made one, stays in your own Drive until you delete it there.

23. Children

The app is intended for business owners and is not directed at children.
